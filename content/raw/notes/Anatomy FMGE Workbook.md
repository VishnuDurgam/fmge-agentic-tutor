Know the Faculty
Dr. Rajesh Kaushal is a distinguished Anatomy faculty and mentor, known for guiding 
PG aspirants, NEET PG and FMGE students since 2001. He is celebrated for his 
unique teaching approach—Human Anatomy Made Simple!—which transforms 
complex topics into intuitive line diagrams and clinically integrated concepts. His 
sessions are structured around conceptual clarity, interdisciplinary relevance, and 
strategic repetition for long-term retention. Dr. Kaushal’s teaching bridges Anatomy 
with Surgery, Medicine, and Obstetrics to meet the evolving demands of competitive 
exams. He is the acclaimed author of Self Assessment and Review of Anatomy 
(Jaypee Publications), now in its 6th edition. With decades of high-impact teaching 
across India and internationally, he continues to be a trusted name in medical 
education. His commitment lies in empowering students with clarity, conﬁdence, and 
competence in Anatomy.
