ORTHOPEDICS 
Chapter I. Introduction to orthopedics 
 
1. What is orthopedics? 
 
• 
Branch of medicine that deals with injuries and diseases of bones, joints, ligaments, tendons 
and muscles. 
 
 
2. Parts of a Long bone 
 
 
• 
Diaphysis is the long tubular middle part of the bone 
• 
Metaphysis is the flared part of bone between the epiphysis and diaphysis 
• 
Epiphysis is the terminal most part of bone 
 
 
• 
Physis or the growth plate is seen in children between the epiphysis and the metaphysis. 
• 
It is responsible for the linear growth of the bone 
• 
Injury to the physis can result in growth arrest and this can lead to shortening or 
deformities of bone

•

• 
Tough bone in the diaphysis is called the cortical bone
