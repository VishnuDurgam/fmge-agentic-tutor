Q1. A patient with microcytic anemia has a raised Total Iron Binding Capacity (TIBC).
This finding is most consistent with:
A. Anemia of chronic disease
B. Sideroblastic anemia
C. Iron deficiency anemia
D. Thalassemia trait
Answer: C
Q2. Pyridoxine (Vitamin B6) is used therapeutically in some cases of sideroblastic
anemia because it is an essential cofactor for which enzyme?
A. Ferrochelatase
B. ALA synthase (ALAS2)
C. Ferroportin
D. Hepcidin
Answer: B
ALA synthase (δ-aminolevulinate synthase) is the rate-limiting, pyridoxine (B6)-dependent enzyme
of the first step of heme synthesis — impaired activity underlies many sideroblastic anemias.
