MENTAL HEALTH CARE ACT 2017
• This act presumes that any person who attempts suicide is having severe stress  and is 
not punished under Sec. 309 IPC (Attempted suicide)
Note: There is no mention about attempted suicide in BNS 
Any person who attempts to commit suicide shall be presumed, unless proved 
otherwise, to have severe stress and shall not be tried and punished under sec. 309 IPC.
The appropriate Government shall have a duty to provide care, treatment and 
rehabilitation to a person, having severe stress and who attempted to commit suicide, to 
reduce the risk of recurrence of attempt to commit suicide.
