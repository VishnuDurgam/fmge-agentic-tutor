# Adding your own study material

Open **My material** in the website (or the installed app) and add anything you are allowed to study from:

- a PDF (typed or scanned; scanned pages are read with OCR automatically),
- photos or screenshots of notes (PNG, JPG),
- a text or Markdown file,
- a link to a web page, a PDF, or a Google Drive file shared as "Anyone with the link".

Progress shows page by page. When a file says **Ready**, you can ask the tutor about it, press **Make 5 questions**, or take the **My material** mock exam.

Notes
- Your uploads belong to your device ID. Other users never see them in answers, practice or mock exams.
- The AI that writes answers and questions is a free cloud model (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter), so the passages used are sent to it. Upload only material you are allowed to use.
- Large scanned PDFs take a few minutes (about 2-3 seconds per page). You can leave the page and come back.
- Questions made from your material are drafts. Check them against the book before relying on them.
- On free hosting the server's disk is wiped when it restarts, which removes uploads. A small paid disk (see DEPLOY.md) keeps them.

For a bulk offline run on a computer you can still use `content/tools/ocr_pdf.py`.
